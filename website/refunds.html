Refunds and cancellation

How to stop Virgue Pro and what happens when a payment needs correcting.

Last updated 31 August 2026.

Cancel your subscription

Open your account, choose Manage billing, and cancel through the Stripe customer portal. Cancellation stops the next renewal; your paid access normally remains available until the end of the current billing period.

Refunds

If you were charged in error, charged more than once, or cannot access a paid feature because of a confirmed service failure, contact support promptly and include the Stripe receipt date and last four digits only. Never send your full card number.

We generally do not refund unused portions of a billing period after a voluntary cancellation, except where required by applicable consumer law or where we agree that a correction is appropriate.

Failed payments

Stripe may retry a failed payment and may update the subscription status. Update your payment method in the Stripe customer portal. Access may change if the payment remains unresolved after any applicable grace period.

Contact

Use the support page for a billing question. Do not post passwords, Roblox cookies, session tokens, or complete payment details.
