Privacy policy

How Valdor handles account, billing, website, and local Roblox workspace data.

Last updated 31 August 2026.

Who operates Valdor

Valdor — Roblox Account Manager is operated by Reece Leneveu under the Valdor name. This policy applies to the Valdor website, account centre, billing API, and Windows desktop app.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, authentication session, and account status.
  • Billing details: Stripe customer, subscription, price, payment status, and entitlement identifiers. Stripe handles card details; Valdor does not receive or store your full card number.
  • Website service data: basic requests, security logs, and hosting data needed to operate the site and billing endpoints.
  • Desktop workspace data: profile metadata, games, categories, settings, session history, and other data you choose to create in the app.

Roblox credentials stay on your device

Roblox cookies and passwords imported into the desktop app are stored locally using Windows secure storage. They are used by the app for Roblox actions and are not sent to the Valdor website, Stripe, or the billing API.

Profile names, game assignments, and session metadata are kept in the app's local data directory. Do not send cookies, passwords, session tokens, or private account data in a support request.

Why we use the data

  • To create and secure your Valdor account.
  • To provide downloads and manage your plan or subscription.
  • To calculate feature access from confirmed Stripe subscription events.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

Services involved

Neon provides authentication and hosted billing data, Stripe processes payments and subscriptions, Vercel hosts the website and API, GitHub hosts public release assets, and Roblox provides the third-party platform the app connects to. Each service handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and requests

We retain account and billing records while they are needed to provide the service, meet accounting obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service. Local desktop data remains on your device until you remove it or use the app's data controls.

For privacy, deletion, or data questions, use the support page. Ask for a private contact route before sharing account details, and never include credentials.
